# Pre-training audit (2026-08-13)

- 0813 raw bags: speed20/25/30; 5894/5876/4319 `/drive` messages. Pose and
  odometry are approximately 50 Hz, IMU approximately 100 Hz. `/teleop` and
  `/lpf_imu` have zero messages.
- Aligned NPZs contain 15 columns and preserve 50 Hz values, but omit original
  source timestamps and topic age. They cannot prove stale-topic or clock-domain
  validity; the raw DB3 files remain the source of truth.
- Centerline-index unwrapping detects 7/8/5 partial-or-complete lap IDs. Laps
  1--5 of speed20/25 are training, their remaining laps validation, and all of
  speed30 is holdout. A 2.4 s purge is applied around split boundaries.
- A model transition maps raw row `t` to `t+2`. Recorded replay consumes
  commands `t` and `t+1` as two 20 ms substeps. Future rollout must hold one
  candidate command for both substeps.
- Existing CUDA uses a single `Params::dt=1/control_rate_hz=0.02` for model
  integration, actuator updates, slew limits, filtering, and costs. It shifts
  `h_prev_controls_` by one at every 20 ms solve. This is incompatible with a
  40 ms knot without a phase-aware shift.
- Existing deployed binary is a 20-64-32-3 derivative residual loaded into CUDA
  constant memory. The new binary has metadata magic `EHSR004`, 34 inputs, and
  bounded 40 ms state corrections; it cannot be loaded by the old enum/path.
- Required production edits after the model-quality gate: separate control,
  model, and history dt; add a new enum/path/metadata parser; implement two
  history substeps; phase-aware warm-start; Python/CUDA 1/5/30/60-step parity;
  cost/time scaling tests; and Orin K=2000,T=60 benchmark.

Smoke result: data build, split/mask, 10-batch training, checkpoint, metadata
binary, Python inference, and 1/5/60-step execution pass. The 100-epoch one-step
candidate fails the 1.2 s speed30 acceptance gate, so CUDA activation is blocked
pending recursive multi-horizon fine-tuning.
